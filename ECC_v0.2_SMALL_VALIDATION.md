## Standards (2 tested)

### S01 — IFRS

Original citation: "IFRS Foundation. IFRS Standards (official consolidated text, current edition)" — no dated version.

**Web-verified finding:** a real, dated "2026 Required IFRS Standards" edition exists, and more importantly, **IFRS 18 (replacing IAS 1 entirely) becomes effective January 1, 2027** — a major, imminent, already-confirmed structural change to financial statement presentation. Annual Improvements Volume 11 also makes specific technical changes affecting IFRS 16 lease modifications, directly relevant to the Knowledge Base's own PR14 (Lease Accounting under IFRS 16).

**Verdict: Currency Check FAIL.** Not because the citation is wrong, but because it's undated and silent about a superseding change that's already confirmed and imminent — exactly the failure mode step 3 of the revised rule exists to catch. [IFRS 2026 update](https://www.ifrs.org/news-and-events/news/2026/01/now-available-ifrs-accounting-standards-required-2026-two-editions/)

### S08 — MiFID II

Original citation: generic "MiFID II" — no dated version, no amendment tracking.

**Web-verified finding:** MiFID II is under active, material revision (MiFID III / MiFIR review). New best-execution RTS were adopted April 2026, entering into force ~Q2/Q3 2026, applying ~Q4 2027/Q1 2028 — directly relevant to PR16 (Best Execution Procedure), which is grounded on exactly this requirement.

**Verdict: Currency Check FAIL.** Same failure mode as S01 — a real regulation actively being superseded, invisible to a citation with no date attached. [MiFID II/MiFIR review status](https://www.esma.europa.eu/trading/mifid-ii-and-mifir-review)

**Both Standards previously marked ECC-Pass now need remediation** — not because the original Evidence was fabricated or wrong, but because "Pass" without a real Currency Check was never actually earned. This is worth stating plainly: the v0.2 Evidence Pilot's Pass verdicts for S01 and S03 stand for *presence and authenticity* of citation, but not for *currency* — that dimension simply hadn't been tested until now.

## Patterns (3 tested)

### PA01 — Momentum Reversal in Taiwan Small-Caps (Provisional, derived-from FA04)

Its basis is your own thesis finding — and per this project's own memory, that finding was **not statistically significant** (H1: ρ=0.5429, p=0.2972). Under the *original* waiver ("1 source sufficient"), this technically passes — a real source exists. Under the **revised** waiver, it does not: a non-significant result is not an affirmative observation of the pattern, it's an inconclusive one. **Verdict: fails the revised Provisional bar.** This is exactly the gap the revision was written to close, confirmed against a real object rather than a hypothetical.

### PA02 — January Effect (Disputed, references FA03)

The *original* rule had nothing to say about Disputed at all. Under the **revised** rule, Disputed requires both a supporting and an opposing source. A supporting source already exists in principle (the classical January-effect literature); a real opposing source was located this round — recent research finds the effect **shrank sharply after the 1980s and is no longer statistically exploitable after transaction costs**, consistent with arbitrage eroding a once-real anomaly. **Verdict: with the opposing source now identified, PA02 can genuinely satisfy the revised Disputed requirement** — the one case in this validation batch that moves toward Pass rather than away from it. [January effect evidence](https://www.evidenceinvestor.com/post/is-there-a-january-effect-in-small-cap-stocks)
