# RP-001 Phase 2A: Hypothesis Verdicts

Verdicts assigned strictly per the pre-committed criteria in `RP001_CONFIRMATORY_ACCEPTANCE_CRITERIA.md`, against the results in `RP001_PHASE2A_CONFIRMATORY_RESULTS.md`. No verdict was influenced by, or changed after, seeing whether it produced a favorable outcome.

| Hypothesis | Verdict | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| **H-C1** — Pre-break positive predictive power | **Not Replicated** | No horizon (t+1/t+3/t+5) reaches NW t>1.96 or BH-FDR q<0.10. Mean IC ≈ 0 at all three horizons, ~40-70× smaller than the exploratory pre-break IC (0.052, t=3.99). |
| **H-C2** — Post-break null effect | **Replicated** (with an important caveat) | `\|mean IC\|<0.01` and `\|NW t\|<1.96` at t+5, meeting the letter of the criterion. Caveat: with H-C1 Not Replicated, there is no established pre-break effect for the post-break period to have lost — a null-vs-null result is far weaker evidence than the pre-registered comparison intended. |
| **H-C3** — Liquidity conditionality | **Partially Replicated** | Direction correct (Illiquid and Mid both exceed Liquid; Liquid not significant), but not clean — only the Mid tercile independently clears significance (t=5.41, q=2.0e-07); Illiquid is directional but not significant (t=1.83, q=0.155). The original "Illiquid ≈ Mid > Liquid" symmetry is not reproduced — full-universe data shows "Mid ≫ Illiquid > Liquid." |
| **H-C4** — Volatility effect is break-conditional | **Not Replicated** | The defining asymmetry (Low-vol & Pre-break significant, Low-vol & Post-break null) requires the pre-break cell to remain significant. It does not (t=0.789) — none of the four double-sort cells is statistically significant at full-universe scale, so there is no significant effect left to test for break-conditionality. |
| **H-C5** — No genuine interaction effects | **Not Replicated** | 4 of 5 testable interactions (F_INT_01, 03, 04, 07) show residual IC surviving both `\|NW t\|>1.96` and BH-FDR q<0.10 after joint residualization against both constituents — the acceptance criteria's "3 or more show genuine significant incremental IC" threshold for Not Replicated is met. F_INT_02/F_INT_06 untestable (Deviation D-08, market cap unavailable). Magnitude caveat: residual ICs (0.0016–0.0067) are 75-95% smaller than each feature's own raw IC — overwhelmingly additive, but not exactly zero at this sample size. |

## What this means for RP-001's overall conclusion

**F_INST_01's core claim — a positive, pre-break-concentrated predictive relationship — does not replicate on the full, survivorship-bias-free universe and full history.** Of the five confirmatory hypotheses, one replicates cleanly by letter (but is weakened by H-C1's failure), one partially replicates, and three do not replicate — including the central pre-break effect (H-C1) and its defining conditionality mechanism (H-C4). H-C5 additionally finds that four interaction features previously characterized as artifacts carry small but statistically robust incremental signal at full-universe scale — a genuine new finding requiring its own follow-up, not evidence in either direction for F_INST_01 itself.

**This is not "the study failed."** It is exactly the outcome confirmatory validation exists to detect: an exploratory finding, built and tested on the same 50-stock, ~2-year sample, that does not survive an independent test on ~30× more stocks and ~7× more history. The Milestone 1D Feature Freeze's own explicit condition — *"Cannot be described as universally stable"* — is, if anything, strengthened by this result, not contradicted by it.
