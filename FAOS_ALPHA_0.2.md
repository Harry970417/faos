## What closes here

**Architecture Era:** Domain Model, Invariants, Taxonomy, Governance — Frozen since ARCHITECTURE FOUNDATION v1.0.
**Knowledge Era:** KOM, Classification, the Knowledge Base itself through two remediation rounds — proven against 100 real research questions (Evaluation Suite v0.1).
**Evidence Era:** Evidence Domain, ECC, three real validation rounds (Pilot v0.1 → v0.2 → Targeted Remediation) — closed by real evidence, not by running out of things to design. PA01's withdrawal is the clearest proof point: the Promotion Gate (Invariant #8) worked exactly as designed, against your own research, without being softened for it.

## New operating rule, effective immediately

**Architecture Foundation, KOM, Classification, Evidence Domain, and ECC do not change by design preference from this point forward.** Any future change to them must be driven by evidence produced during actual Research Production — a real research task that surfaces a real structural gap — the same standard ECC itself was held to before its Freeze. No new governance models, no speculative redesign, no "this would be cleaner" refactoring.

## What's next

Research Production has no content yet — it needs a real task. What's the first one: a specific piece of your graduate research, an investment analysis, a research report, something else? FAOS is ready to be used, not designed.
