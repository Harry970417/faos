## Integrity caveat, stated up front

Every citation below is a real, verifiable work I'm confident exists and is correctly attributed (author, year, title, venue) — these are foundational, extremely well-known texts in finance, not obscure or uncertain claims. What I have **not** verified to precision: exact volume/issue/page numbers or DOIs. Citing those with false confidence would be worse than omitting them. Treat this pilot as author/year/venue-verified, locator-pending — a final pass before anything is called publication-grade should confirm exact bibliographic details against the original source.

## One honest finding this pilot itself surfaced: triangulation risk

**9 of 20 objects rest on exactly one Evidence source:** ME07, S01, A01, T03, C08, T09, FR01, T06, S03. A single citation, however authoritative, is weaker than corroborated sources — this is true even where the one source is excellent (e.g. T03 Modern Portfolio Theory resting solely on Markowitz 1952). Not a defect requiring immediate fixing, but the honest baseline for what "complete evidence" actually means before declaring the pilot fully mature — worth a second source for at least the highest-centrality of these nine (T03, A01, C08) before treating this batch as the model for how bulk population should work.
