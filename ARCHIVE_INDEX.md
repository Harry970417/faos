## What is deliberately NOT archived, despite carrying a "v0.1" or "v0.2" suffix

Several `_v0.2`-suffixed RP-001 documents (`RP001_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY_v0.2.md`, `RP001_METHODS_AND_DATA_v0.2.md`, `RP001_RESULTS_TABLES_v0.2.md`, `RP001_LIMITATIONS_v0.2.md`, `RP001_REPRODUCIBILITY_APPENDIX_v0.2.md`, `RP001_FAOS_TRACE_v0.2.md`, `RP001_CHANGELOG_v0.2.md`, `RP001_RESEARCH_FINDINGS_v0.2.md`) remain in `research/` as **current, active documents** — no later document duplicates their specific content (detailed methods, results tables, limitations, reproducibility appendix, FAOS trace, or changelog). Archiving them on filename pattern alone would have deleted the only source for that content from the discoverable tier. Same reasoning for several FAOS-level `v0.1`/`v0.2` documents (e.g. `architecture/KNOWLEDGE_GRAPH_AUDIT_v0.2.md`, `evidence/ECC_v0.2_REVISION.md`) — these are the current canonical versions with nothing newer to supersede them, not an old iteration of something still evolving.

## What is NOT in this archive because it was never superseded

The exploratory-phase RP-001 documents (Milestones 0A–1D, `research/RP001_FAOS_TRACE.md`, `FEATURE_REGISTRY.md`'s Milestone 1D table, etc.) remain in their functional folders, not archived — they are the historical record RP-001's own governance explicitly preserves, and are still the authoritative source for "what the exploratory phase actually found," a question nothing else answers.
