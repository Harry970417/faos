# RP-001 Figure Index

All 11 figures in `figures/`, generated by `research/rp001_phase2a_build_charts.py` directly from the confirmatory dataset, test-results JSON, and acquisition manifests. **No illustrative or placeholder data anywhere.** Re-run the script to regenerate byte-for-byte (no randomness in any chart). See `FIGURE_MANIFEST.md` (repo root) for the compact source/usage table.

## Figure01_UniverseCoverage.png

**Question answered:** How much of the acquired universe actually has usable panel data, by market? **Key finding:** TWSE and TPEx both convert acquisition → eligible panel rows near-completely; the "UNKNOWN" (delisted) segment loses more, expected since delisted stocks often lack retrievable full history. **Use in:** `research/RP001_PHASE2A_FINAL_DATASET.md`, data-scale sections of any report. **Misreading to avoid:** this is a snapshot of stock *counts*, not row counts or time coverage — don't read bar height as "amount of data."

## Figure02_MissingnessDistribution.png

**Question answered:** How is institutional-data coverage distributed across stocks, and where does the 80% gate fall? **Key finding:** Bimodal-ish distribution with a strong right-mass near 100% coverage and a long left tail of sparse stocks — the gate cuts through a real, not arbitrary, break in the distribution's shape. **Use in:** `research/RP001_PHASE2A_CONFIRMATORY_DATASET.md`, `research/RP001_LIMITATIONS_v0.2.md`. **Misreading to avoid:** the gate (69.8% pass rate) was fixed before this distribution was computed — don't read this chart as post-hoc threshold tuning.

## Figure03_InstitutionalCategoryHistory.png

**Question answered:** When did the market-wide Dealer-category schema change, and how does that relate to the 69 individually-verified per-stock exceptions? **Key finding:** A clean, near-total market-wide cutover around Dec 2014 — the per-stock exceptions (D-05) are a minority pattern layered on top of an otherwise clean transition, not evidence the transition itself was messy. **Use in:** `research/RP001_PHASE2A_DEVIATION_LOG.md`, data-quality sections. **Misreading to avoid:** the orange break-window band shows where the *2025 confirmatory* break interval sits — it is unrelated in time to the 2014 Dealer schema change; the two are juxtaposed only because both are "structural date" concepts.

## Figure04_RollingIC.png

**Question answered:** Does F_INST_01's predictive power show the clean pre/post-break pattern the exploratory study found, at full-universe scale? **Key finding:** No — the 60-day rolling IC oscillates around zero across the full 14-year history with no visible regime shift at the locked break point. This is the single most direct visual evidence for H-C1's Not Replicated verdict. **Use in:** `portfolio/RP001_FINAL_SHOWCASE.md`, `portfolio/RP001_PORTFOLIO_ONE_PAGE.md` (the single most representative chart for a one-page portfolio). **Misreading to avoid:** don't read local peaks (e.g. around 2024-2025) as "the effect was real then" — no sub-period in this chart was tested for significance individually; only the pre/post split at the locked point estimate was a pre-registered test.

## Figure05_BreakBeforeAfter.png

**Question answered:** How large is the exploratory-vs-confirmatory gap in break-period IC magnitude? **Key finding:** Confirmatory pre-break IC is ~47x smaller than the exploratory estimate; confirmatory post-break IC is small and slightly positive (vs. exploratory's small negative). **Use in:** `research/RP001_RESULTS_TABLES_v0.2.md`, exploratory-vs-confirmatory comparison sections. **Misreading to avoid:** the hatched bars are not "the effect weakened" — at full-universe scale the pre-break bar itself is not statistically significant, so this is closer to "the effect was not there" than "the effect got smaller."

## Figure06_LiquidityGroups.png

**Question answered:** Does the Illiquid/Mid > Liquid pattern from the exploratory study hold at full-universe scale? **Key finding:** Directionally yes, but asymmetric — only Mid clears significance; Illiquid is directional but marginal. **Use in:** H-C3 discussion in any report. **Misreading to avoid:** don't read this as full replication — the exploratory pattern was "Illiquid ≈ Mid," this shows "Mid ≫ Illiquid," a materially different shape, hence the "Partially Replicated" verdict rather than "Replicated."

## Figure07_HypothesisVerdicts.png

**Question answered:** At a glance, what happened to each of the five hypotheses? **Key finding:** 3 Not Replicated, 1 Partially Replicated, 1 Replicated-with-caveat. **Use in:** any executive summary, the one-page portfolio, interview prep. **Misreading to avoid:** the green "Replicated*" cell (H-C2) has an asterisk for a reason — read the caveat before citing this as a clean success in any context.

## Figure08_ExploratoryVsConfirmatory.png

**Question answered:** Across five representative statistics, how do exploratory and confirmatory magnitudes compare, including sign flips? **Key finding:** Three of five show large magnitude collapse (H-C1, H-C3, H-C4); two (interaction residuals) show a sign flip from small-negative to small-positive-and-significant. **Use in:** `portfolio/RP001_FINAL_SHOWCASE.md` section K, the "unexpected findings" narrative anywhere. **Misreading to avoid:** the two rightmost bars are the H-C5 finding, not H-C1-H-C4 — don't conflate "small residual survives" with "the main effect survives."

## Figure09_InteractionResidualization.png

**Question answered:** For each of the five testable interaction features, how much does residualization change the IC, and does the residual survive significance testing? **Key finding:** 4 of 5 residuals flip sign relative to raw IC and clear |NW t|>1.96 (starred); F_INT_04 does not flip sign but remains significant; only F_INT_05's residual is small even though it also clears the raw t-bar. **Use in:** H-C5 discussion, `research/RP001_RESULTS_TABLES_v0.2.md` Table 5. **Misreading to avoid:** a starred bar means "statistically survives," not "economically large" — every residual bar is 75-95% smaller than its corresponding raw bar; read bar height in the context of the raw-IC bar right next to it, not in isolation.

## Figure10_DataQualitySummary.png

**Question answered:** At a glance, how much data-quality investigation actually happened during acquisition? **Key finding:** 69 individually-verified Dealer-schema instances (zero affected the core factor), 16,459 mis-dated rows caught by the Trading Calendar Gate, 504 legitimate empty API responses correctly distinguished from failures, 2 real tooling bugs found and fixed. **Use in:** data-engineering-focused portfolio materials (e.g. `applications/RP001_APPLICATION_NTUST_FINANCE.md`, `applications/RP001_APPLICATION_TAIPEITECH_IMFIN.md`). **Misreading to avoid:** these are counts of *investigated and resolved* issues, not counts of *remaining* problems — the chart's subtitle says "zero unresolved" for a reason, don't present it as a list of open defects.

## Figure11_ResearchLifecycleTimeline.png

**Question answered:** What happened when, across the full RP-001 lifecycle? **Key finding:** The confirmatory phase (Protocol Lock to closure) spans about 3.5 weeks of actual work, most of it concentrated in the final acquisition-to-closure push (2026-08-01 to 2026-08-03). **Use in:** methodology/process sections, application materials emphasizing project-management capability. **Misreading to avoid:** dates are commit dates, not necessarily continuous working time — this is not a claim about hours worked per day.
